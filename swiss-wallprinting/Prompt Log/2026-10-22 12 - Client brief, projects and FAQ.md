## Decisions

- **The private message stays private**: a public repo must not carry the client's notes on domain access, the previous developer, and costs. The public spec keeps only what the site uses. For the same reason the prompt above links to it instead of quoting it.
- **Gallery → Projects** (a tab and URL rename, the key `projects`): the client calls it "projects" in his buttons and his gallery is project stories; the photo grid stays below them. The old URLs were never public.
- **FAQ as a tab**, not only a home section: the client lists it as a part of the site, and three of four reference sites have one. The home page stays as it is.
- **The configurator stays first** in the hero buttons (the author's surprise); the client's two buttons follow.
- **Placeholders instead of mock photos** for the projects: a mock photo under a real client's name would be wrong. The projects are still mock, so the launch waits for the photos.
- **Nothing invented in the FAQ:** no duration, price, or preparation details that the client did not give; "it depends, we tell you with the quote" instead.
- **Durability wording unchanged** (the client's old claim) but flagged (Q14), not quietly softened.
- **TikTok, opening days, `info@`:** not added until the client sends them.
